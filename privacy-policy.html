This is a plain-language summary of how RecallAware handles your information. It is written to be accurate about what the app actually does, not boilerplate — if you'd like more detail on anything here, contact us (see the Support page).

The short version

RecallAware does not require an account, a login, or your name. There is no server-side record of who you are or what you're watching for. Your watchlist, scan history, and alert history all live only on your own device. The only personal information RecallAware ever asks for is an optional backup email address, and only if you choose to add one.

What RecallAware collects

Information that stays on your device and is never sent to us

  • Your watchlist (which countries, categories, hazard types, allergens, brands, or products you've chosen to watch)
  • Your scan history ("Digital Pantry") — what you've scanned and when
  • Your past-alerts history — a local log of push notifications your device has received
  • Your onboarding and app preferences

All of the above is stored locally on your phone using standard on-device storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it. We have no copy of it and no way to retrieve it.

Information sent to our server

  • Barcodes you scan or enter — sent to our server to look up whether that product has a matching recall. We do not store a history of what you've scanned tied to any identity; the lookup is answered and not retained against you personally.
  • An optional backup email address, only if you choose to add one in the app. If you do, we store that email address along with the categories, hazards, and countries you've asked to be notified about, so we can send you a backup recall alert by email in case a push notification doesn't reach you. We never sell, rent, or share this email address with anyone else, and we don't use it for marketing. You can ask us to remove it at any time — see Support.

Push notifications

RecallAware uses Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications. Your device subscribes directly to Google's infrastructure for the topics matching your watchlist (for example, a category or country). Google's systems know which topics a device is subscribed to in order to deliver the notification; our own server does not keep a registry of devices or what any individual device is watching. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Camera / barcode scanning

RecallAware uses your device's camera only to scan a barcode while the scan screen is open. The camera image is processed on your device to read the barcode number — nothing from the camera itself (no photo or video) is stored or sent anywhere. Only the barcode number is sent to our server, for the recall lookup described above.

What we don't do

  • We don't require sign-in, and we don't have a system for one today.
  • We don't collect your location.
  • We don't run advertising, and we don't use advertising trackers or ad SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
  • We don't build a profile of you beyond the optional email/watchlist pairing described above.

Recall data sources

The recall information shown in the app comes from public government sources (see the home page for the full list). That data is public recall information, not personal information about you.

Children's privacy

RecallAware is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with an email address, contact us and we'll remove it.

Data retention and deletion

On-device data (your watchlist, scan history, alert history) is deleted the moment you uninstall the app. If you've added an optional backup email address, you can ask us to delete it at any time by contacting us — see Support — and we will remove it from our records.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because the only personal information we hold is an optional email address (and only if you've provided one), exercising these rights is as simple as contacting us to remove it — see Support.

Changes to this policy

RecallAware is actively being developed, and this policy will be updated if what the app collects or how it's used changes — for example, if an account system or additional features are added later. We'll update the "Last updated" date above whenever that happens.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request to remove your email address? See the Support page for how to reach us.

Note: this policy describes RecallAware as it actually works today. It isn't a substitute for legal advice — if you're relying on this for a business or compliance decision, it's worth a quick review by an actual lawyer, particularly given users outside the US.
