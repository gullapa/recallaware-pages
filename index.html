What RecallAware does

RecallAware watches for product recalls that match what you've told it to watch for — by category, hazard type, allergen, brand, product, or country — and lets you know as soon as one shows up, without you having to go check anywhere yourself. You can also scan a barcode any time to check a specific product on the spot.

Recall data comes from public government sources, including the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, the FDA, USDA FSIS, UK Food Standards Agency and Office for Product Safety and Standards, Health Canada, the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission, France's RappelConso, and OECD Global Recalls.

Currently in beta

RecallAware is in a small closed beta right now. If you're testing it and run into a bug, have feedback, or a recall source you'd like added, see the Support page for how to reach us.
