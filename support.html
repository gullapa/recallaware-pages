Delete your data (RecallAware, by Northbook Software)

RecallAware stores your watchlist (the categories, hazards, and countries you've chosen to follow) on your own device. The only piece of data that ever leaves your device and reaches our server is your optional backup email address, if you've chosen to add one under Profile so you can get alerts by email as well as by push notification.

To delete your data, do either of these:

  1. In the app, go to Profile and remove your backup email address (tap the field, clear it, and save). This immediately deletes your email address and its associated alert preferences from our server. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device (your watchlist and dismissed-alerts history).
  2. Or email ajmidjourney@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my RecallAware data" from the email address you subscribed with. We will remove it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted: your backup email address, and the category/hazard/country preferences and alert cadence tied to that address on our server.

What's kept: nothing tied to you personally — we don't have accounts, device IDs, or any other identifier linked to your email. Public product recall records (sourced from government agencies like CPSC, FDA, USDA/FSIS, and others) are retained indefinitely, but they were never your data — they're public safety records we mirror for the app to work.

Official government sources

RecallAware is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government agency. All recall data shown in the app is sourced from these agencies' public feeds and is provided for informational purposes only — always verify against the original source:

  • U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)
  • U.S. FDA — food, drug, and medical device recalls
  • USDA / FSIS — meat and poultry recalls
  • Health Canada
  • UK Food Standards Agency
  • UK Office for Product Safety and Standards
  • France — RappelConso
  • Australia — ACCC / Product Safety Australia

Testing RecallAware right now?

If you're part of the beta, the fastest way to report a bug is through your testing app's own feedback tool — it automatically attaches a screenshot and basic device info, which helps us fix things faster:

  • iOS (TestFlight): take a screenshot while using the app, or open TestFlight → RecallAware → "Send Beta Feedback."
  • Android (Play testing): use the feedback option in the Play Store listing for the test track, or just email us directly.

You can also always just email us at the address above.

Common questions

Why didn't I get an alert for something I'm watching?

Push notifications require notification permission to be turned on for the app (check your phone's system Settings, or the gear icon inside RecallAware). If permission is on and you still think something was missed, pull down to refresh the home screen, or email us with the product/recall in question.

Is my data safe?

See our Privacy Policy — in short, almost everything stays on your own device.
